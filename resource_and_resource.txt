Drum Practice Pro	drumpracticepro.com	Very drummer-focused. Visual drum sequencer, time signatures, subdivisions, metronome/count-in, drum notation, grooves and interactive drum-theory guides. Great bridge between theory and actually programming/playing a groove. D Drum Practice Pro
Erhythm	erhythm.org	Excellent visual rhythm laboratory. Circular representations of rhythm, odd meters, polyrhythms, Euclidean rhythms, multi-track percussion and notation. Particularly interesting for understanding rhythmic structure visually.
MusicTheory.net	musictheory.net	Excellent encyclopedia/reference for note values, rests, measures, time signatures, simple/compound/odd meter, dots and ties. Less drum-specific but extremely useful as a lookup resource.
Complete Rhythm Trainer	Complete Rhythm Trainer	One of the most comprehensive rhythm-reading/training systems. 252 progressive drills covering simple/compound/asymmetric meters, triplets, swing, quintuplets, polyrhythms, two-voice rhythms, dictation and more. C Complete Rhythm Trainer
Tunvox Rhythm Trainer	Tunvox Rhythm Trainer	Read a rhythm, hear the grid, then tap it back. Gives early/late/in-time feedback. Covers 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 6/8 and 9/8 plus subdivisions, rests and triplets. T Tunvox
MusioMo	MusioMo	Interactive music-theory site with a dedicated Rhythm Training section, time signatures, note values, rhythmic patterns, metronome and virtual drum kit. M MusioMo
TimeArt	TimeArt	Broad interactive music-learning resource with illustrated theory, rhythm/time-signature material, quizzes, sight reading and a sophisticated metronome.
Artform Rhythm Trainer	Artform Rhythm Trainer	Read → hear → tap. Progressive rhythm exercises with timing feedback, including syncopation, triplets, sixteenths, rests and two-voice rhythms.

MusicReason – Polyrhythm Trainer	MusicReason Polyrhythm Trainer	Very clean tool for understanding two pulse streams occupying the same cycle. Particularly good for seeing the mathematical relationship between ratios such as 3:2 and 4:3. M MusicReason

Metron.me	Metron.me	Very useful browser metronome/reference tool: simple, compound and odd meters, subdivisions, accents, swing, polyrhythm, tap tempo and tempo trainer. M Metron.me
Titan Audio – Metronome	Titan Audio Metronome	More advanced metronome with odd meters, subdivisions, swing, offbeat accents, up to four polyrhythmic voices, silent-bar practice and speed training. T Titan Audio
Cloud Atelier – Rhythm Trainer	Cloud Atelier Rhythm Trainer	Simple and useful tap-the-notation-back trainer. Random one-bar rhythms, count-in and timing scores. Also has a Time Signature Explorer. C Cloud Atelier
